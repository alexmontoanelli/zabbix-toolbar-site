---
title: Updates
description: How Zabbix Toolbar keeps itself up to date.
order: 7
---

Zabbix Toolbar is updated through the Mac App Store, like any other app. To get new versions automatically, turn on *App Store → Settings → Automatic Updates*.

Your installed version is shown in **Settings → General → About → Version**.

*Coming from the direct-download version (1.0.x):* the App Store version imports your servers and settings the first time it opens. Tokens and passwords aren't carried over — enter them again in each server's settings.
